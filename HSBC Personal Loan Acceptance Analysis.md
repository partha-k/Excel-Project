## Project Overview:

This project transforms HSBC's marketing approach by revealing which customers are most likely to accept personal loans. Using comprehensive data analysis in Microsoft Excel, the project identifies key acceptance drivers and delivers actionable recommendations to improve marketing efficiency, reduce wasted spend, and maximize campaign returns.

## Key Insights:

1. High-income segment displayed dramatically superior acceptance behavior.
2. Graduate and professional education strongly predicts loan acceptance.
3. TD account holders exhibited exceptionally high conversion potential.
4. High monthly CCAvg customers demonstrated greater loan acceptance.
5. Age, Experience, Online platform usage, Credit Card Membership and Securities Account Holding had weak individual influence on acceptance.

## Business Recommendations

1. Prioritize high-income customers(100K+/year).
2. Target Graduate \& Professional customers.
3. Focus on TD Account holders for higher Conversion Rates.
4. Use high CCAvg Spending Customers for campaign targeting
5. Reduce marketing efforts toward low-conversion customer segments.
